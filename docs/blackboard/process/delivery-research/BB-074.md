# BB-074 — Controlled delivery-harness value benchmark research

Status: RESEARCH RESET — 2026-09-27.

BB-074 no longer treats a DIRECT-vs-ExHarness matched pilot as the complete value experiment. It must consume the neutral substrate/harness contracts from BB-065/077/081 and add the lifecycle scenarios needed to measure ExHarness as a delivery harness.

Canonical contract: `docs/blackboard/process/harness-benchmark-program-research.md`.

## Research question

Does the supported ExHarness delivery profile improve real software-delivery economics and reliability at comparable independently accepted quality, and which parts of that result are attributable to harness behavior rather than model choice or benchmark artifacts?

## Two workload classes

### 1. External/public harness workload

Purpose: external validity and comparability.

Use the fixed-factor contract from BB-077. Hold model snapshot, task/prompt, workspace, budget, artifact extraction and evaluator constant. Include a minimal/direct control and ExHarness profile. Report complete attempt accounting.

### 2. ExHarness delivery-lifecycle workload

Purpose: measure capabilities a coding pass-rate benchmark does not capture.

Predeclare scenarios such as:

- verifier rejection followed by bounded repair;
- stale context/currentness rejection;
- process crash and resume;
- session/device handoff;
- provider timeout/429/UNKNOWN usage;
- CI failure and repair routing;
- dependency not DONE;
- research finding invalidates implementation premise;
- claim takeover/stale-worker fencing;
- multi-session long-horizon continuation.

The direct/minimal arm does not need to emulate ExHarness internals. Where a lifecycle capability has no direct analogue, report the capability outcome and its resource/human cost explicitly instead of inventing a fake symmetric workflow.

## Result profile

Report at least:

```text
accepted quality
cost per accepted delivery
tokens per accepted delivery
lead time
human active minutes
review / repair / coordination effort
repeat consistency
fault robustness
recovery success
failure fingerprint
```

Do not emit one opaque "ExHarness score". Use paired evidence and uncertainty where meaningful. A result may show a trade-off rather than a winner.

## Human-effort measurement

BB-074 must research an auditable human-effort protocol before READY. Distinguish:

- active review/coordination/repair time;
- passive waiting/provider time;
- benchmark operator/setup time;
- product decisions that remain intentionally human-owned.

Do not infer human effort from model/tool events alone.

## Relationship to BB-075 and BB-076

BB-075 selects one HOW candidate from an observed failure fingerprint, bottleneck or ablation result and evaluates it on held-out work under the same frozen contract.

BB-076 reproduces the supported delivery behavior and benchmark profile on the exact release. A green CI, successful agent exit or one favorable metric is insufficient.
