## Discriminating experiment

Executed research evidence:

- `docs/blackboard/evidence/BB-090/gate-gap-probe.mjs`
- `docs/blackboard/evidence/BB-090/gate-gap-probe-result.json`

Negative cases the Worker must prove by executed tests:

- A plan at or above the threshold with negative cases but no negativeCaseBindings fails readiness materialization before any provider call.
- A binding set that leaves a negativeVerificationCases index uncovered or covers one twice fails assertPlan.
- A binding whose testRef is not in sourceSeams.expectedTests, or whose verificationId does not map to its criterionId, fails assertPlan.
- A bound title absent from its verification log, marked failed (spec ✖ or TAP not ok), or appearing twice fails evidence collection.
- A title that only appears as a substring of another test title does not satisfy the binding.
- A bound test body that does not invoke subjectSymbol fails SUBJECT_NOT_INVOKED; the real BB-064 fail-closed test bound to createOracleContextResolver reproduces this.
- A bound test body containing `|| true)`, `assert.ok(true` or no assertions fails VACUOUS_ASSERTION.
- Plans below the threshold or without the field (all current DONE/READY plans) continue to validate and publish unchanged.
- Any write under docs/blackboard/artifacts/**, docs/blackboard/evidence/** or packages/** fails repository scope verification.
- A verification run whose name pattern selects zero tests must fail (node --test exits non-zero on no matching tests is not assumed; the NB suite asserts each prefix has at least one test).
