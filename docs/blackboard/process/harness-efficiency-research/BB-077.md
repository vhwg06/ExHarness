Status: RESEARCH RESET — 2026-09-27.

The previous READY matched synthetic protocol is revoked. BB-077 must first define how ExHarness will measure the harness itself as an experimental variable. Worker implementation is not authorized by the historical readiness judgment.

Canonical contract: `docs/blackboard/process/harness-benchmark-program-research.md`.

## Research question

Under the same model, task, prompt, starting workspace, resource ceiling, artifact extraction and evaluator, what quality/efficiency/failure behavior is attributable to the harness rather than the model or benchmark environment?

## Evidence to use

- Harness-Bench (arXiv:2605.27922): shared environments, budgets and evaluators; artifact/trace/usage/validator capture.
- Claw-SWE-Bench (arXiv:2606.12344): fixed prompt/runtime/workspace/patch/evaluator harness comparison.
- The Scaffold Effect in Coding Agents (arXiv:2607.22585): fixed-model harness comparisons with large efficiency and failure-pattern variation.
- Harbor (`harbor-framework/harbor`): preferred neutral execution/evaluation substrate candidate.
- mini-SWE-agent and OpenHands SDK remain eligible control/reference implementations.

External results motivate the protocol. They do not establish ExHarness performance.

## Minimum arm design

Research must support at least:

```text
fixed benchmark contract
      |
      +-- minimal/direct harness
      +-- current ExHarness synchronous Core
      +-- future ExHarness candidate profile
```

If the neutral substrate cannot host one arm without changing its semantics materially, document that as a benchmark limitation rather than hiding the difference.

## Metrics

Do not optimize or report pass rate alone. Predeclare a multidimensional profile:

- independent accepted quality;
- cost and tokens per accepted task;
- model turns;
- tool/capability calls;
- no-progress turns/loops where observable;
- elapsed latency;
- provider wait separately from active execution;
- repeat consistency;
- failure fingerprint by harness;
- incomplete/UNKNOWN accounting.

Report paired distributions/uncertainty where the sample permits it. No best-of-k cherry-picking or success-only filtering.

## Dataset/workload research

The old FANOUT/DEPENDENT_CHAIN synthetic pair is not automatically canonical. Research must choose:

1. a small calibration set compatible with BB-065;
2. an external/public workload for harness external validity where practical;
3. a held-out portion that is not consumed while tuning candidate harness behavior.

Task choice must be justified by what harness mechanism it discriminates. Short tasks that saturate at 100% are acceptable calibration but poor evidence of long-horizon harness value.
