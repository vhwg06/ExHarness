# BB-081 — Held-out harness ablation and robustness acceptance research

Status: RESEARCH RESET — 2026-09-27.

The previous four-task held-out READY protocol is revoked. BB-081 now researches how the async candidate will be accepted under the unified harness benchmark program. Worker acceptance/publication is not authorized until a fresh readiness judgment exists.

Canonical contract: `docs/blackboard/process/harness-benchmark-program-research.md`.

## Research question

After BB-078/079/080 exist, can their effects be attributed separately and does the resulting supported profile improve the quality-cost-latency-recovery frontier without weakening effect safety?

## Required ablation

Research the exact held-out design for:

```text
A = current synchronous Core
B = A + BB-078 detached operations
C = B + BB-079 cache-stable result context
D = C + BB-080 steering / wakeup / recovery
```

The same model/task/prompt/workspace/budget/artifact/evaluator contract applies to every arm. If a capability cannot be toggled independently after implementation, BB-081 must specify another attribution method before READY rather than reporting only bundled D-vs-A results.

## Robustness dimension

Predeclare repeated runs and fault injection including:

- provider timeout / 429 / ambiguous completion;
- tool failure;
- process kill after durable operation state;
- restart/resume;
- cancellation;
- duplicate wakeup/completion;
- external-effect ambiguity and replay fencing.

Measure accepted quality, cost/tokens, latency, duplicate effects, recovery result and failure fingerprint together.

## Acceptance semantics

`PROMOTE_ASYNC` versus `KEEP_SYNC_BASELINE` is not a pass-rate-only decision.

Research must define a preregistered Pareto/non-inferiority gate:

- quality/safety cannot regress beyond the declared bound;
- cost/token/latency improvements are measured, not assumed;
- recovery and duplicate-effect behavior must satisfy explicit safety gates;
- missing/misaligned evidence yields INCONCLUSIVE;
- all attempts/retries/failures remain in the denominator and accounting.

The benchmark does not become correctness authority. EffectOperation truth, Application authority, QA and Jev authority remain unchanged.
