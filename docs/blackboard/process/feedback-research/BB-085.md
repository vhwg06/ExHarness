# BB-085 — Implement application-owned feedback episode lifecycle

Status: RESEARCH RECORD for a READY plan. The canonical contract is `docs/blackboard/artifacts/ready-implement-plan/BB-085.json`; this document is not implementation authority and adds no claims beyond the objective, plan and readiness judgment.

## Outcome

A durable, application-owned feedback episode lifecycle turns grounded finding input into an authorized response and a fresh measured outcome, ending in an explicit FeedbackResolution.

## Current gap

BB-083 research mixed the per-episode feedback lifecycle with observation productization and cross-episode self-improvement. The lifecycle itself needs only grounded observation input and application authority; it must not wait on optimizer, benchmark or HOW promotion semantics.

## Discriminating experiment

No task-local probe was executed. The design rests on the delivered sources and on the planned contracts listed under Handoff. The Worker must establish the negative cases below by execution.

Negative cases the Worker must prove by executed tests:

- An episode whose groundedInputRef digest mismatches the stored input, or that references a non-GroundedFindingInput artifact, is rejected.
- MEASURED impact with any UNRESOLVED input, or with an input that fails consumption-time currentness recheck, is rejected.
- ACTED on an episode with UNRESOLVED or STALE inputs, without actionRefs or without a frozen outcome policy is rejected; REJECTED without rationaleRef, DEFERRED without revisitCondition and SUPERSEDED without successorEpisodeRef are rejected.
- A principal of kind OBSERVER, EVALUATOR, PROVIDER or OPTIMIZER, a principal that principalAuthority does not verify, or a principal field embedded in episode, finding or observation payload cannot create, revise or resolve.
- Replaying the same respond call after success returns CONVERGED and leaves the head revision unchanged; a stale expectedRevision with different content fails STALE_REVISION without rebase.
- A crash after artifact put but before compareAndSwap leaves the head unchanged; retry produces the same artifact ref and one transition; recover ignores the orphan artifact.
- An outcome bound to a non-current response, a fresh input already in the episode, an execution attempt present in the baseline inputs or a boundary not strictly after effectiveAfterBoundary is UNKNOWN.
- A missing or null required measurement, or an UNRESOLVED fresh input, is UNKNOWN and never IMPROVED; a missing measurement is never coerced to zero.
- Changing the outcome policy after the ACTED response is impossible: recordOutcome uses only the policy digest frozen in the response.
- resolve() with UNKNOWN outcome or DEFERRED state fails; a caller-supplied resolution value different from the derived value fails; RESOLVED accepts no further transition.
- Lifecycle modules importing how-evolution, @exharness/benchmark, optimizer, pattern or BB-083 feedback-*.js code fail the dependency import-graph test.
- Worker start with BB-084 not DONE, missing delivery receipt/Living ref or missing package-root export exits PLAN_INPUT_CONTRADICTION before product writes.

## Required product evidence

- **FL1**: FeedbackEpisode binds exact GroundedFindingInput refs+digests, finding with producer identity, MEASURED or HYPOTHESIZED impact, scopeKey and contrary evidence; MEASURED impact and ACTED are refused when any input is UNRESOLVED or fails consumption recheck. Evidence: Contract tests for identity/digest binding and forbidden authority fields; controller tests refusing MEASURED/ACTED on UNRESOLVED or STALE input.
- **FL2**: Only a principalAuthority-verified application principal can respond or resolve; OBSERVER/EVALUATOR/PROVIDER/OPTIMIZER principals and payload-embedded principals are rejected; transitions use expected-revision CAS, exact replay returns CONVERGED, stale revision fails without rebase and crash recovery never duplicates a transition. Evidence: Authority negative tests, CAS/replay/stale tests, crash-between-put-and-CAS and fresh-process recovery tests.
- **FL3**: A recorded outcome is IMPROVED, NO_CHANGE, REGRESSED or UNKNOWN from the frozen outcome policy and D8 freshness identity; missing measurement, UNRESOLVED or non-fresh evidence, or a non-current response is UNKNOWN. Evidence: Table-driven classification boundaries for both directions and freshness negative tests.
- **FL4**: FeedbackResolution is terminal, derived from head state, never produced from UNKNOWN or DEFERRED, and the lifecycle modules import nothing from HOW evolution, benchmark, optimizer or pattern code. Evidence: Resolution derivation tests, terminal-state tests and an import-graph assertion in the dependency test.
- **FL5**: The implementation binds only DONE BB-084 package-root exports through the preflight, writes only authorized files and documents delivered feedback-lifecycle truth in its Living Doc. Evidence: Preflight manifest output, forbidden-write check and Living Doc diff.
