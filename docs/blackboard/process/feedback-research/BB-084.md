## Outcome

Runtime execution evidence becomes a grounded, context-bound, fail-closed observation that downstream feedback can consume without re-deriving causal truth or context.

BB-083 research specified Observation and ContextBinding inside a feedback package, but observation productization is a distinct capability: it normalizes BB-058 causal reconstruction and delivered execution identity, binds BB-064 Oracle context/currentness, and classifies missing, stale or ambiguous context before any feedback semantics exist.

## Discriminating experiment

No task-local probe was executed. The design rests on the delivered sources and on the planned contracts listed under Handoff. The Worker must establish the negative cases below by execution.

Negative cases the Worker must prove by executed tests:

- Observation with a missing or mismatched subject digest, attempt binding digest or attestation digest is rejected.
- A provider event whose attemptBindingRef differs from the observation's execution binding is rejected; a provider event id alone cannot establish attempt identity.
- A BB-058 MISSING_PROVENANCE fact stays MISSING_PROVENANCE; narrative text cannot clear it or add a PROVENANCED fact.
- Changing only narrative does not change observationId; changing any fact, evidence digest or subject pin does.
- Unmeasured values are null with a reason; a zero default for a missing measurement is rejected.
- Resolution status UNSATISFIED, or PARTIAL with a REQUIRED unresolved evidence item, classifies as UNRESOLVED with UNSATISFIED_CONTEXT or MISSING_CONTEXT.
- A binding without a receipt classifies as CURRENTNESS_UNVERIFIABLE; the binder never self-certifies CURRENT.
- receiptCurrentness returning STALE at binding time classifies STALE_CONTEXT; returning STALE at consumption time makes assertGroundedFindingInputCurrent return UNRESOLVED without rewriting the stored input.
- An observation bound to a ContextResolution for a different requirement or subject classifies SUBJECT_MISMATCH.
- GroundedFindingInput containing finding, impact, disposition, response, verdict, accepted, promoted, remediation or principal at any depth is rejected.
- Passing a port with publish, claim, dispatch, accept, recover or compareAndSwap methods to the projector or binder is rejected at construction.
- A historical-subject rebuild after newer canonical heads returns the original ids; a CURRENT query after the transition produces a new subject id and never mixes facts.
- Worker start with BB-058 or BB-064 not DONE, missing delivery receipt/Living refs, missing package-root export or a required deep import exits PLAN_INPUT_CONTRADICTION before product writes.
- Candidate writes under packages/oracle/**, BB-058 owner files, BB-083 feedback-*.js paths or docs/blackboard/** fail repository scope verification.

## Required product evidence

- **OB1**: GROUNDED_OBSERVATION_V1 binds exact subject pin, executionAttemptId, runtimeInvocationId, attempt binding and attestation refs+digests and provider events as attributes; every fact carries evidence refs or MISSING_PROVENANCE, and narrative is UNTRUSTED_NARRATIVE excluded from identity and facts. Evidence: Tests for exact identity/digest binding, forged or unbound provider event rejection, MISSING_PROVENANCE pass-through, narrative exclusion and deterministic observationId.
- **OB2**: OBSERVATION_CONTEXT_BINDING_V1 binds resolutionId, requirementId, status, receipt and injected currentness, and classification returns GROUNDED only under D8 with typed UNRESOLVED reasons for missing, unsatisfied, stale, ambiguous, unverifiable or mismatched context. Evidence: Table-driven classification tests covering every reason, PARTIAL with REQUIRED vs OPTIONAL unresolved, missing receipt and STALE currentness.
- **OB3**: GROUNDED_FINDING_INPUT_V1 is the only feedback-facing output; it rejects finding/disposition/authority keys at any depth and consumption-time currentness recheck turns STALE or unverifiable into UNRESOLVED without mutating the stored input. Evidence: Forbidden-key, consumption recheck and immutability tests.
- **OB4**: Observation modules are read-only: write-capable ports are rejected, no authority or head state is persisted, and a fresh process rebuild from the same pinned subject yields identical identities while historical and current subjects never mix. Evidence: Port-rejection tests, fresh node process rebuild comparison and historical-vs-current subject test.
- **OB5**: The implementation composes only DONE BB-058/BB-064 package-root exports through the preflight, modifies no forbidden owner file, and documents delivered observed-implementation truth in its Living Doc. Evidence: Preflight export/receipt manifest output, forbidden-write/deep-import repository check and Living Doc diff.
